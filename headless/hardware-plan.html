Sequencing

1. Validate on the CM4 IO Board do this firstConfirm USB-host-to-iPhone pairing, hostapd AP, and the headless server end to end before spending on a board spin.
2. Minimal carrier PCBJust the blocks above — no unnecessary ports/peripherals from the IO board.
3. Enclosure + antenna tuningVerify Wi-Fi range once everything is inside a case.
4. ComplianceFCC/CE for the intentional radiator (Wi-Fi) + digital device rules; battery certs only if you add one.
This plan targets the B2B/QA-testing framing discussed earlier (app developers validating geofenced/location-aware features), not a consumer "spoof your GPS" product — that distinction matters for both legal exposure and Apple's Developer Agreement.
